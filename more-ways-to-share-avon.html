More Ways to Share Your Avon Business

Running an Avon business is all about connections — reaching people where they already spend their time and building trust through visibility. Explore free online platforms that can help you connect with customers.

Google My Business

Helps your Avon business appear in local search and on Google Maps, with photos, reviews, opening hours and a shop link.

Tip: Add testimonials, your Avon shop link and regular brochure updates.

Nextdoor

Connect with nearby customers, build local trust and share relevant community offers or events.

Tip: Participate naturally and follow local promotion rules.

Facebook Groups

Reach local shoppers and beauty communities, share brochures and recommendations, and build relationships.

Tip: Read each group's rules before posting promotions.

Instagram

Share makeup, skincare, product demonstrations, Reels, Stories and personal recommendations.

Tip: Use consistent visuals and relevant hashtags.

Reddit

Learn what customers want and participate in skincare and beauty discussions.

Tip: Offer useful advice rather than spam or hard-selling.

YouTube

Create tutorials, product reviews and demonstrations that people can discover over time.

Tip: Include your Avon shop link in video descriptions.

X (formerly Twitter)

Share quick offers, product tips and join beauty conversations.

Tip: Balance promotional posts with genuine interaction.

Tumblr

Create an aesthetic Avon blog with photos, GIFs, beauty inspiration and product stories.

Tip: Keep a consistent style.

Twitch

Try live makeup tutorials, product demos or Get Ready With Me streams.

Tip: Be conversational and authentic.

Other platforms

Pinterest for beauty boards and product pins; TikTok for short videos; LinkedIn for professional networking.

Tip: Choose the platforms you can use consistently.

💖 Final Thoughts

Promoting your Avon business online isn’t about being everywhere at once — it’s about being consistent where it counts. Each platform helps you reach a different type of audience:

  • Google for local discovery,
  • Facebook for community connections,
  • Instagram and YouTube for visual appeal,
  • Reddit and X for conversation, and
  • Pinterest and TikTok for creative inspiration.

By spreading your Avon presence across these spaces, you’re not just selling products — you’re building relationships, trust, and a personal brand that people remember.
