About PrettyUs

About Prettyus

Prettyus is my independent Avon beauty space, created to help customers shop genuine Avon products with confidence. I am an Independent Avon Representative in the UK, and I personally run Prettyus to offer honest advice, friendly support, and a more personal service alongside a trusted beauty brand.

When you shop through Prettyus, you are supporting a small independent business while still receiving official Avon products.

Why I started Prettyus

I have always appreciated beauty products that are affordable, reliable, and easy to use. Avon has long been a brand I trust, and becoming an independent representative gave me the opportunity to share those products in a more personal and supportive way.

Prettyus was created as a simple, welcoming place where customers can shop Avon with confidence while still having access to personal help when needed.

How ordering & delivery works

I generally recommend direct delivery orders, as these are packed, dispatched, and delivered directly by Avon to your door.

Please note that Avon may charge a delivery fee on smaller direct delivery orders, while larger orders may qualify for free delivery. This option is often best for customers placing bigger orders or wanting a simple, hands-off delivery service.

Local orders – Hailsham & Hellingly

If you live in Hailsham or Hellingly, you also have the option to place an order through Prettyus using my local order form.

Local orders placed through me do not include a delivery charge, which can make this option especially useful for smaller orders. I personally manage these orders and arrange delivery or collection where appropriate. Please note that delivery arrangements may sometimes be limited due to health-related circumstances.

Local ordering through my form is available only to customers in Hailsham and Hellingly.

Commission & transparency

I earn commission from Avon once a required sales level is reached. This applies to both direct delivery and local orders.

Every order supports me and helps me continue offering personal assistance, supporting local customers, and running Prettyus as an independent business. Thank you for choosing to shop with me.
