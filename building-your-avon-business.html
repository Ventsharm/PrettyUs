Building Your Avon Business

Everything you need to know to start, grow and succeed with your Avon business

Introduction

Starting an Avon business gives you the chance to earn in a flexible way, in your own time, and in a way that suits your lifestyle. You can build your business online, in person, or by combining both.

As an Avon Representative, you can set up your own online store and share products with customers. When customers choose direct delivery, Avon handles the delivery and you can still earn on those sales.

Avon campaigns change regularly, which means there are always new products, new offers, and fresh opportunities to share with customers.

Your earnings and percentage levels can change depending on your sales performance and Avon’s current structure, so it is always best to check the latest information in your Avon account and Avon ON.

With consistency, effort, and the right support, Avon can grow from a side income into something much bigger.

How earning works

You can earn up to around 32% commission on the products you sell, depending on your sales level each campaign.

As your business grows, there is also the opportunity to earn an additional 3%–8% from team sales if you choose to build a team.

As you grow your sales, you may move through different earnings or commission levels. Avon’s structure can change, so your account dashboard is the best place to check your current progress and the latest qualifying details.

Many representatives build their earnings through a mixture of regular customer orders, direct delivery, brochure sharing, social media promotion, and repeat customers.

The stronger and more consistent your sales become, the stronger your business becomes too.

Important: always rely on the latest Avon information inside your rep tools, as percentages, thresholds, and reward rules can change over time.

Building your own business

One of the best things about Avon is that it is your business in your own time. You choose how you want to promote it, where you want to share it, and how much effort you want to put into growing it.

You can work from home, share links online, speak to local customers, use brochures, build a social media presence, and create a business style that feels natural to you.

The more effort, planning, and consistency you put in, the more potential your business has to grow.

Recruiting and team building

Recruiting can also become an important part of growing your Avon income. When you build a team and support new representatives properly, it can open the door to additional earnings and leadership opportunities.

It is important to support your team well, encourage them, and help them stay active. A strong team is built through guidance, patience, and consistency.

Team-related earnings and leadership rewards can vary depending on Avon’s current structure, so always check your latest leadership information inside your Avon tools.

Sales leadership

Sales leadership is for representatives who want to go beyond personal selling and help build and guide a team. Avon’s leadership criteria can change, so the latest information in your Avon account should always be treated as the most accurate source.

In general, leadership growth is based on a combination of your own sales, team activity, and the number of active representatives in your team.

Even if one month is quieter than another, that does not mean your business cannot keep growing. Avon is not a get-rich-quick scheme, but with enough time, effort, and consistency, it can become a meaningful source of income.

Success stories

There are people who have built very successful Avon businesses over time. While results are never guaranteed, success is possible for those who stay committed, learn the system properly, and keep improving the way they promote their business.

The most important thing is to stay realistic, stay consistent, and keep growing step by step.

Using a domain name

Buying your own domain name can make your business look more professional and easier for customers to remember. A simple and memorable domain can then be forwarded to your Avon store.

This can help make your business feel more established and can give customers a smoother experience when finding you online.

If you are serious about growing your Avon business, a domain name can be a worthwhile investment.

Business cards

Business cards are still a lovely and effective way to promote your business. They are ideal for local advertising, introducing yourself to potential customers, and leaving your details in a simple and professional format.

Even in an online world, printed cards can still be a useful part of your marketing.

Facebook advertising

Creating a Facebook page for your Avon business can help you reach more people and make your business look more established. You can post offers, new products, reviews, updates, and helpful content for customers.

If you want to reach more people, Facebook adverts can also be worth exploring. Even a small budget can help introduce your page to new local customers or people interested in beauty and shopping.

Google Maps

Adding your business to Google can help new people find you more easily. It can give your business more visibility and may help you look more trustworthy and established.

You do not need to make your home address public if you are not comfortable doing that. Always choose the safest and most suitable option for your business.

Sharing your business

It is important not to rely on just one platform. The more places you share your business professionally, the more chances you have of reaching the right customers.

You can share your Avon business through social media, local groups, community pages, direct messages, brochures, word of mouth, and other suitable places online and offline.
