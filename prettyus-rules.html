PrettyUs Rules

PrettyUs is a landing page for all my Avon content — including products, offers, and my journey as a representative 💄

It also includes a discussion area where users can chat and connect. The rules below apply to all discussions and community interactions 🌸

⚠️ Illegal posts

Any illegal content or activity posted in the discussion area will be removed as soon as it is identified. Users who post such content will be permanently banned.

💬 Be respectful

Please treat other members kindly. No bullying, harassment, hate speech, threats, or abusive behaviour will be allowed in discussions.

🌸 Keep PrettyUs safe

Do not post scams, harmful links, spam, adult content, or anything that could put other users at risk. If you see anything that breaks the rules, please report it to help keep PrettyUs safe.

Thank you for respecting PrettyUs 💕
